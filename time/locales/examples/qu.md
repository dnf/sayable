## Clock time formatting

| --------------- | ---------------------------- | -------- | ------- |
| Midnight        | chawpi tuta                  | 12:00 AM | 12:00a  |

| 5 past 9        | isqun pichqa                 | 9:05 AM  | 9:05a   |

| Half past 11    | chunka hukniyuq kimsa chunka | 11:30 AM | 11:30a  |

| 5 to 6          | suqta mana pichqa            | 5:55 PM  | 5:55p   |
